AI Stack

All AI calls route through a single orchestrator (app/services/ai_orchestrator.py) — no module talks to a provider directly. This gives routing, fallbacks, rate limiting, cost tracking, and audit logging in one place.

Validated models

  • nemotron-super-49b-v1 — Q=93.6, best for M1/M3/M4
  • llama-3.3-70b-instruct — Q=91.3, best speed/quality
  • kimi-k2-instruct — Q=91.2, fastest, ES/EN/ZH native, Q=100 on M2/M5

Provider fallback chain

  • 1. NVIDIA NIM (free, 40 RPM)

Why ComplianceOS

ComplianceOS is not a generic compliance dashboard. It is AI-native regulatory intelligence and compliance operations infrastructure for LATAM regulated industries.

Enterprise features

  • Multi-tenant architecture, JWT authentication, API keys
  • Immutable, hash-chained audit logs
  • RAG retrieval & graph intelligence
  • Crawler scheduling, SSE events
  • Observability + Prometheus metrics
  • Webhook support
  • Workflow orchestration + predictive intelligence

What sets it apart

  • Regulation is parsed into structured, machine-readable obligations — not just searchable text
  • Continuous monitoring with a deterministic rule floor, so AI failures never fail open ("no anomalies")
  • Every compliance decision is audit-logged through a tamper-evident hash chain
  • Built LATAM-first: jurisdiction logic, regulators, and language are native, not bolted on
  • Pharmacovigilance is a first-class vertical, not an afterthought bolted onto a financial-AML core
